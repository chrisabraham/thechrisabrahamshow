## How an article is published (Nov 18, 2026)
The source of every article is `data/articles/<slug>.html`: a `key: value` header (title, seo_title, description, date, author, image, image_alt, image_credit, card, keywords, about), a `---` line, then the HTML body. Run `python3 build.py --offline` and commit what it writes. Never hand-write or hand-edit `articles/*.html`, `articles.html`, `articles.xml`, `rss.xml`, `atom.xml`, `sitemap.xml`, or `llms.txt`: the daily GitHub build regenerates all of them from `data/articles/`, so anything that exists only in the built files disappears the next morning. That is why 22 articles kept dropping off the index until their sources were recovered on November 18, 2026. Commit every file a page references (its image, its `-600.jpg`, its narration MP3) in the same push; the build fails on a missing image. Article images are raster files, JPG or PNG; an SVG doodle is kept beside its rasterized JPG, which the page uses. Search metadata: `seo_title` is about 60 characters at most and `description` is 160 characters at most, both written as summaries, never truncated; the on-page title can be as long as the piece needs.
