FORCE / FORCING

In magic, "forcing" refers to the act of making a spectator choose a particular card, object, or number without them realizing that they were influenced or coerced to make that choice. For example, a magician might use a particular sleight of hand technique to make it appear as though a spectator freely chose a card, when in reality the magician had already predetermined the outcome.

NUDGE / NUDGING

Nudging involves presenting choices and information in a way that encourages people to make certain decisions, without overtly telling them what to do.
