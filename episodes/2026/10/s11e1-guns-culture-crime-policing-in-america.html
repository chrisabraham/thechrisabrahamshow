Welcome back to Season 11 of The Chris Abraham Show! In this season premiere, host Chris Abraham dives headfirst into the fierce philosophical and cultural debate surrounding gun ownership, policing, state power, and personal responsibility in modern America.

Spurred by a recent episode of Oprah Winfrey's podcast—Why Is America Different Than Every Other Country When It Comes to Guns?—Chris reflects on why American attitudes toward crime and guns are fundamentally different from those in Europe and other developed nations. Drawing on his experience living in Berlin under ubiquitous law enforcement, Chris contrasts Europe’s high-trust, collectivist culture with the American ethos of radical self-reliance, concealed carry, and constitutional freedom. This episode explores the core societal divide: Is the government your caretaker, or is individual self-defense your ultimate safeguard?

In This Episode:

  • The High-Trust vs. Low-Trust Divide: Comparing European civic trust and heavy police presence in cities like Berlin with American skepticism of state authority and the "you're on your own" mindset.
  • Personal Firearm Evolution: Chris shares his personal journey as a gun owner—from getting a Glock 23 on his 40th birthday to concealed carry training, micro-32 handguns, and contemplating the J-frame .38 revolver.
  • The Second Amendment as a Safeguard: Why the Second Amendment serves as the ultimate backup "canary in the coal mine" for American liberty ("my canary packs heat").
  • Tech & AI Backstory: How Chris built and launched thechrisabrahamshow.com in under an hour using Claude Code on Linux Mint and Digital Ocean.

BUY THE BOOK! The American Way of Killing: The Invention of an Epidemic by Malcolm Gladwell

Malcolm Gladwell—one of America’s foremost thought leaders—examines our country’s obsession with guns and the devastating toll gun violence has taken on our communities. In his new book, The American Way of Killing, Malcolm challenges some of our most deeply held beliefs about American gun violence: what we’re missing about the root cause of most violence, how modern medicine is obscuring the reality of the problem, why the answer might be more police—not less—and how we’ve lost sight of the historical context surrounding the Second Amendment. Oprah and Malcolm speak with a Chicago-based E.R. doctor who lost 15 close friends and family members to gun violence. He teaches students as young as fourth grade how to treat a gunshot wound. This episode also offers some common sense solutions to a seemingly intractable problem.
