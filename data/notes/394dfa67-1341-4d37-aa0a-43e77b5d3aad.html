Glossary

Ampelmann
The little hatted man on East German, and now all Berlin, pedestrian lights: green and walking, or red with his arms out. Cross against him and Berliners will tell you you're setting a bad example for the children. More.
High trust, low trust
My shorthand for the difference between countries where people treat the state as a caretaker and the police as theirs, and America, where the state is presumed an adversary and "nobody's coming to save you."
Panopticon and Flock cameras
The panopticon is Jeremy Bentham's prison where every cell can be watched from one tower; now shorthand for surveillance everywhere. Flock sells the license plate cameras spreading through American towns. More.
"Better judged by twelve than carried by six"
Gun-owner proverb: better to face a jury of twelve for defending yourself than be carried to your grave by six pallbearers.
Constitutional carry
Carrying a concealed handgun without a permit. Most states now allow it; many still issue permits, which are useful for carrying in other states that honor them (reciprocity). More.
Concealed carry permit
A license to carry a hidden handgun, usually after training and a background check. Concealed means concealed: nobody's supposed to know.
Glock 23, 19, 17
Austrian polymer pistols. The 23 is the compact in .40 S&W, my first gun, bought on my 40th birthday in 2010. The 17 is the full-size 9mm and the 19 the compact 9mm, often called the most popular handgun in America. Mine are old police trade-ins from D.C. More.
Red dot, RMR
A small reflex sight mounted on a pistol's slide. The Trijicon RMR is the common police choice.
J-frame
Smith & Wesson's small five-shot revolver frame. The 340 PD is a featherweight J-frame in .357 Magnum that also shoots .38 Special, the classic pocket gun.
Kel-Tec P32, "fluff and buff"
A tiny, light .32 ACP pocket pistol. "Fluff and buff" is the owner's ritual of polishing its internals to make it run reliably.
Seecamp LWS-32
A tiny, expensive, hand-fitted .32 ACP pistol, about as small as a serious handgun gets. Mine is serial number CHR1S.
Kydex and the MIC holster
Kydex is a tough heat-formed plastic, also used for aircraft interiors, and the standard holster material. A MIC holster is just a Kydex trigger guard cover on a cord: draw, and the cord pulls it off.
Stop the Bleed kit
A tourniquet, pressure bandage, and gauze for stopping heavy bleeding until help arrives. Part of the "nobody's coming to save you" kit, along with Narcan.
"To protect and to serve"
The LAPD's motto since the 1950s, painted on its cars. My view: police exist to keep law and order and respond to antisocial behavior, not to protect you personally.
FAFO
"F*** around and find out." The bluntest statement of American personal-responsibility absolutism.
The backup canary
Miners carried canaries because they die of bad air before people do. My first canary for American freedom was the First Amendment; I think it's sick, so my backup is the Second. "My canary packs heat." More.
